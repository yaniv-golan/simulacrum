# F3 assessment protocol

**Bar.** A player who did not build the machine can say *why* it failed, from the post-mortem
alone, without reading code or telemetry.

**Fixtures.** Use failures whose cause is **independently known** to the assessor and not visible
on screen — e.g. a joint deliberately under-rated, a power path deliberately broken. At least
three, of different causes.

**Procedure.** The participant did not build the machine and has not seen the fixture before. Show
the post-mortem. Ask: what failed, and why?

**Scoring.** A pass requires identifying the **cause**, not repeating the sentence on screen.
"The joint broke" is not a pass if the post-mortem says the joint broke; "the joint was carrying
more than it was rated for because the load moved when the arm extended" is. Record the
participant's words verbatim in the notes.
