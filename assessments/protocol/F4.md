# F4 unassisted-play assessment protocol

Use a target player aged 14–17. Record their stable participant identifier, prior exposure, and the build identifier displayed by the running application. Let them play for ten minutes without assistance. Do not explain what the interface wants them to do.

Pass requires at least one observed moment of visible delight and no moment of confusion about what the application wants. Record concrete actions, timestamps and the participant's words that support both judgments. Mere task completion or an assessor's expectation of delight is insufficient. Record failures and any assistance in the session log.
